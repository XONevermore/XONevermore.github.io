Dark Side: Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Dark Side is a game made by MyMax. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (stardust, Hangar upgrades, best stages and scores) is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between runs at most every few minutes, and videos you choose to watch for a reward (revive, double stardust, new skill cards). To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices on the game's title screen.

What we do not collect

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting or notifications, does not read your contacts, files, photos, camera or microphone, and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

Dark Side is not directed at children under 13.
